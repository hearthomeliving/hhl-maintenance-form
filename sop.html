Maintenance Pipeline

How we report, assign, close and rate maintenance jobs at Hearth & Home Living. Written so anyone can follow it, including someone brand new.

Emergency? Do not use the form first.

Fire, gas smell, flooding, a break-in, or a child in immediate danger: call 999 or the on-call manager straight away and make the home safe. Log it on the form afterwards so there is a record.

Ratings and compliance run themselves in the background.

You are the reporter

1. Report the job

  1. Open the form on your phone: hearthomeliving.github.io/hhl-maintenance-form. Save it to your home screen so next time it is one tap.
  2. Answer one question at a time: your home, who you are, your name, and the category that fits. No category fits? Pick Other.
  3. Do the pre-checks it asks for (reset the boiler, check the fuse, check the pressure). Tick them off honestly. This saves needless call-outs and money.
  4. Add photos. At least one wide shot of where the problem is, plus close-ups.
  5. Submit. You get a reference starting MAINT-. Screenshot it, that is your case number.

What happens automatically

  • A task appears on the Non Routine Maintenance list within seconds.
  • Ofsted-relevant jobs (heating, hot water, fire safety, doors, damp, fences) are flagged to prioritise.
  • Vehicle problems go to the Vehicles list instead.
  • Nobody at the home needs to do anything else. The ball is with the Maintenance Team.
You are the Maintenance Team

2. Triage and assign

  1. Open the Non Routine Maintenance list. New jobs land at the top as "Open".
  2. Read it, check the photos and the pre-checks, then set Severity: Critical, High, Medium or Low.
  3. Pick from the Approved Contractor dropdown (not the old "Contractors" field, which is being retired). Only vetted contractors appear.
  4. Tick Send Follow-Up. Never tick this before you have added the contractor, that is what fires the emails.

What happens when you tick Send Follow-Up

  • Insurance is checked first. If expired or missing, the contractor is not emailed and the Manager gets a red-banner "pick another and try again".
  • If valid, the contractor is emailed the job, the Manager's contact, the Deputy as backup and all photos. The Manager gets a follow-up form link.
  • The task's Follow-Up Status moves to "Sent".
The contractor

3. The visit

  1. They get an email: "Maintenance job assigned: MAINT-... at [home]", with the Manager's and Deputy's contacts.
  2. They open the photos to understand the problem before travelling.
  3. They ring or message the House Manager to agree a time.
  4. They turn up and do the work.

Contractor and House Manager coordinate directly. The Maintenance Team only steps in if asked.

You are the House Manager (or Deputy)

4. Close the loop and rate

  1. Open the follow-up email and tap the teal Open follow-up form button.
  2. Choose one: Mark Complete (done), Add Notes (partly done or an update, stays open), or Escalate (something went wrong, type what and the team is emailed at once).
  3. If you marked complete, answer four quick questions (below), then tap Confirm complete. The task closes and the team gets the summary.
The four rating questions
  1. Did the contractor arrive on time? (Yes / No)
  2. Fixed on the first visit? (Yes / Partial / No)
  3. Standard of work? (Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor)
  4. Would you use them again? (Yes / With reservations / No)
Answer honestly. This is how we know who our good contractors are.

Running in the background

You do not need to do anything for these.

  • Nightly contractor scorecard (03:00). Every rating rolls into the contractor's record (on-time %, first-visit fix %, standard, would-use-again, sample size over six months), visible on the Approved Contractors list.
  • Daily compliance email (08:30). The team is emailed any contractor whose insurance has expired, is missing, or is due within 30 days. If all are compliant, no email.
  • Planned: a Sunday Home-Manager digest, category SLA chasers, and a contractor visit-date proposal flow.

Reference and troubleshooting

When the job does not fit any category ("Other")

The form makes the reporter do more, on purpose: six safety-screening questions (child in the area, immediate danger, structural, gas smell, flooding, shock risk), at least two photos, and a description of 15 words or more. They cannot submit without all three.

Maintenance Team

  1. Read the safety answers first. Any "yes", treat as Critical or High until proven otherwise.
  2. Set Severity manually. Ofsted-relevant is not auto-ticked for "Other", so decide yourself.
  3. If the same kind of "Other" comes up more than twice in a couple of months, tell Adrian and it becomes a proper category.
  4. Assign: if an Approved Contractor covers it, assign as normal. If not, either add a new contractor via Ruth (preferred), or do a one-off manual dispatch (email them yourself, do NOT tick Send Follow-Up, and add a comment "manual dispatch, contractor: [name/phone/email]" for the record).

If "Other" was picked but it clearly fits a category, just rename the task and set the correct category field on it.

Compliance and insurance

Every Approved Contractor needs a current public-liability certificate on file and an expiry date, or the system will not send them jobs.

  1. Open the contractor's task on the Approved Contractors list.
  2. Upload the PDF into "Insurance certificate".
  3. Fill in "Insurance expiry" from the certificate, and save.

DBS status sits on the same task (Not required / Enhanced / Standard / On file / Expired). Update it when paperwork changes. Genuinely exempt trades: leave expiry blank; the daily chase flags it for Ruth to mark.

Duplicates and recurring problems

Same problem reported twice while the first is open: keep the older task, close the newer one, and comment "also reported by [name] on [date]".

A problem comes back within 30 days of closing: rename the task with a [RECURRING] prefix and tell the team. Recurring issues point at a root cause, not just a symptom.

Troubleshooting
No follow-up email
Check Spam for maintenance@hearthomeliving.co.uk and mark Not Spam. Still nothing? Ask the team to re-tick Send Follow-Up.
Tapped the wrong button
Ring the team to reopen the task. Do not resubmit the form for the same job.
Contractor never shows
Wait 24 hours, try once more, then tap Escalate. A reminder also fires automatically after five working days.
Ticked Send Follow-Up, nothing happened
Fill in the Approved Contractor field first. Still nothing? Tell Adrian and check the n8n Automation Error list.
Link says the task is already closed
Someone else completed or escalated it. Check the task to see who did what.
Wrong contractor or wrong home
Change the field on the task; re-tick Send Follow-Up for a new contractor (ring the old one, they are not auto-cancelled).

A job is "done" when

  • The House Manager has tapped Mark Complete on the follow-up form.
  • Four ratings are recorded on the Ratings log.
  • The task shows status "complete".
  • The completion email has reached the Maintenance Team.

If any one is missing, the job is not done.
