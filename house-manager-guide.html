The Maintenance Form

A quick guide for House Managers and staff. Report anything that needs fixing in your home, in about 2 minutes on your phone.

1 Get the app on your phone

You only need to do this once. After that, the Maintenance app sits on your home screen like any other app.

Go to this link on your phone:

https://hearthomeliving.github.io/hhl-maintenance-form/

📱iPhone

  1. Open Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Go to the link above.
  3. Tap the Share button at the bottom (a square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add in the top right.

The blue "Maintenance" icon is now on your home screen.

🤖Android

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Go to the link above.
  3. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  4. Tap Install app (or Add to Home Screen).
  5. Tap Install when asked.

The blue "Maintenance" icon is now on your home screen.

What the form will ask you

  1. Who and where. Pick your home. Add your name, your role, and the best way to reach you (WhatsApp, phone, or email).
  2. Safety check. Is anything happening right now that's dangerous? If yes, tick it. The form tells you what to do (usually: call 999 or your Manager).
  3. Quick checks. Have you tried the obvious things (plug in, switched on, tripped fuse)? Tick what you've done.
  4. Describe the issue. Type in plain English: "door jammed", "leak in bathroom", "boiler not firing". The form suggests matching categories. Tap the one that fits.
  5. Safe checks from the manual. Tick each check you've done. Tap "Can't do" if you couldn't, and add a reason.
  6. Urgent flags. Anything that makes this more urgent (safety concern, child affected). Tick what applies. The maintenance team decides how urgent from here.
  7. The details. Write a short description. Pick which room(s). Set when you first noticed it. Add a photo.
  8. Check and submit. Review the summary. Tap Submit.

3 The photo

Every report needs at least one photo. The maintenance team uses it to decide what tools or parts to bring, and whether they need a contractor.

✓ Do this Frame the photo on the broken thing only. Get close enough to see the fault. Good light helps.
🚰 The dripping tap, close up.
✗ Not this No children in shot. No documents with names on. No medication labels. No pictures of the whole room if a close-up would do.
🚫 A wide shot with a child in the background.

Photos travel outside the home. They live on the ClickUp task and in the maintenance team's inbox. Keep them tightly focused on what needs fixing.

4 If it's an emergency

Do not use this form as your first action in an emergency. Call 999 or your on-call manager first. The form is not a replacement for the emergency phone call.

Call 999 for:

  • Fire or smoke in the building
  • Gas smell that won't clear
  • Structural collapse (ceiling, wall, floor)
  • Flooding creating electrical danger
  • Someone seriously hurt or trapped

Call on-call manager for:

  • Property can't be made secure
  • Smashed window a child could reach
  • Major water leak you can't stop
  • Fire alarm system down, no detection
  • Child on a life-critical medical device with power out

The form's first screen catches these situations. If you tick any of them, the form logs the emergency automatically and tells you the right numbers to call. But you still need to make the phone call yourself.

5 After you submit

  • You get a confirmation email with a reference number (looks like MAINT-2026-1234).
  • The maintenance team gets an alert with your photo attached.
  • The request appears in the maintenance queue for triage.
  • Someone may contact you for more information.
  • If you need to chase, reply to the confirmation email with the reference number.

6 Common problems

The photo won't upload

Usually a signal problem. Try again on WiFi.

The form won't open

Pull the screen down to refresh. If that doesn't work, close the app and reopen it.

I can't find the right category

Type what's wrong in your own words (like "door locked" or "no hot water"). The form will suggest matches. If none fit, tap Use 'Other' and describe it fully.

I lost my reference number

Check your email inbox. The confirmation is titled "Maintenance request received" and has the reference in the subject line.

The Settings button asks for a password

Settings is for admin use only. If you tapped it by accident, tap Cancel and it closes. Ignore it for normal reports.
